Our story

Founded in 2010, Aeroluxe began with a clear brief: aircraft-grade detailing with the same discipline as maintenance. A small team of aviation enthusiasts became a trusted partner for private owners and fleet operators.

Every airframe is treated with the same standard — vintage or current-generation — because the finish has to hold up in flight, not only under hangar lights.

To redefine aircraft detailing through precision and uncompromising quality, so every aircraft we touch reaches its full aesthetic and protective potential.
